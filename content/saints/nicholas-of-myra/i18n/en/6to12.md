Long ago, in a sunny town called Patara by the sea, there lived a kind couple named Theophanes and Nonna. For many years they prayed for a child, and at last God gave them a baby boy. They named him Nicholas, which means "victory of the people."

Nicholas grew up loving God. While other boys played in the marketplace, he liked to go to church, listen to the readings, and learn to pray. His uncle, who was the bishop of the town, saw how good and wise the boy was, and when Nicholas grew up, he made him a priest.

### The secret gift

When Nicholas's parents died, they left him a lot of money. But Nicholas did not want to keep it for himself. He remembered that Jesus said to share with the poor, and he liked to do it **secretly**, so that only God would know.

In his town lived a poor father with three daughters. He was so poor that he could not give his girls a dowry, the gift a family gave when a daughter got married, and in those days that meant they could not marry at all. The father was so desperate that he began to think about doing something very wrong to get money.

[[scene:gold]]

Nicholas heard about this family. One dark night he crept up to their house and tossed a bag of gold coins through the open window. Then he ran away before anyone could see him! In the morning the father found the gold and cried with happiness. Now his eldest daughter could marry.

Nicholas did it again for the second daughter. The third time, the father hid and waited. When the bag of gold came flying through the window, he ran after the stranger and caught him. "It's you, Nicholas!" he cried, and thanked him again and again. But Nicholas asked him to keep it a secret. Many people believe that this is where the custom of secret gifts on St Nicholas's Day comes from.

### Calm on the sea

Nicholas wanted to visit the Holy Land, where Jesus had lived. He set off on a ship. The sky was clear, but Nicholas told the sailors, "A big storm is coming." Soon the wind howled, the waves rose like mountains, and the sailors were terrified. They begged Nicholas to pray.

[[scene:storm]]

Nicholas prayed to God, and the wind and the waves grew still. That is why sailors all over the world still pray to St Nicholas when they are in danger.

### Bishop of Myra

When Nicholas came home, God led him to the city of Myra. The bishops there needed a new archbishop, and God told the oldest bishop in a dream: "The first man who comes to church in the morning, named Nicholas — he is the one I have chosen." Early the next morning, before anyone else, humble Nicholas walked into church to pray. So he became the archbishop of Myra!

He was a loving shepherd to his people. He gave away food and clothes, visited the sick, and comforted the sad. When the Roman emperor began to persecute Christians, Nicholas was put in prison, but he never gave up his faith. When the good emperor Constantine set the Christians free, Nicholas came home to Myra, and everyone cheered.

Nicholas also went to the great Council of Nicaea, where bishops from all over the world gathered to say clearly what Christians believe: that Jesus is truly God, the Son of God. Nicholas stood up bravely for the true faith.

### Saving three innocent men

One day, three innocent men were about to be punished with death because a dishonest governor had taken a bribe. People ran to Nicholas: "Father, help!" Nicholas hurried to the town square. The executioner had already raised his sword!

[[scene:three-men]]

Nicholas grabbed the sword right out of the executioner's hand and set the three men free. Then he told the governor that he had done a terrible wrong. The governor was ashamed and asked for forgiveness.

Three army generals saw it all. Later, back in the capital, these generals were themselves falsely accused and thrown into prison. They prayed, "God of Nicholas, help us!" That night Nicholas appeared to the emperor in a dream and said, "These men are innocent. Set them free!" The emperor did, and he sent gifts to Nicholas's church to say thank you.

### Still helping today

St Nicholas lived to be very old. Even after he died, people kept asking for his prayers, and God did many miracles through him. One story tells of a boy named Basil who was kidnapped and taken far away. On St Nicholas's Day, while his parents were praying, the saint carried him all the way home — still holding the cup he had been serving from!

St Nicholas teaches us to give to others quietly, to stand up for people who are treated unfairly, and to trust in God's help. Holy Nicholas, pray to God for us!
