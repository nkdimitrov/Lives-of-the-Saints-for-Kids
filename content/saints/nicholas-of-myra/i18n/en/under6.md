Nicholas loved God very much. He loved to pray, and he loved to help people.

[[scene:gold]]

A poor daddy had three girls and no money. One dark night, Nicholas tiptoed to their house. *Whoosh!* He threw a bag of gold through the window and ran away. It was a secret gift!

[[scene:storm]]

Once Nicholas sailed on a ship. A big storm came. The wind went *whooo!* and the waves went *splash!* Nicholas prayed, and God made the sea calm and quiet.

[[scene:three-men]]

Nicholas became a bishop, a shepherd for God's people. He was brave. When three good men were in trouble, he ran and saved them.

St Nicholas shows us how to share and to be kind. Holy Nicholas, pray for us!
