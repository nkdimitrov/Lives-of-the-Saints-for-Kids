Anthony lived in Egypt, near a big river with palm trees. One day in church he heard Jesus' words: "Give to the poor, and come, follow Me."

[[scene:gospel]]

So Anthony gave away everything he had to poor people. Then he went to live in the quiet desert, so he could pray to God all day.

[[scene:fortress]]

Sometimes Anthony felt scared in the dark. But he prayed, and God's light came and chased the scary things away. "I am always with you," said Jesus.

[[scene:raven]]

Once Anthony visited another holy hermit named Paul. A black bird flew down with a loaf of bread in its beak — dinner for both of them!

Holy Anthony, pray for us!
