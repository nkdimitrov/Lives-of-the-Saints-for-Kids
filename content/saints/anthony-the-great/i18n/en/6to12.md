Anthony was born in Egypt, in a village near the great river Nile, about 1,750 years ago. His parents were rich Christians, and they took him to church every Sunday. Anthony loved church. He listened carefully to every word of the Bible and remembered it.

When Anthony was about eighteen, his parents died. Now he was in charge of his little sister, a big farm and a lot of money.

### One Gospel verse changes everything

One day, walking to church, Anthony was thinking about how the Apostles left everything to follow Jesus. When he walked in, the priest was reading the Gospel, and Anthony heard Jesus say: **"Go, sell what you have and give it to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven. Then come, follow Me."**

Anthony felt as if Jesus were talking straight to him! He went right out and gave his fields to the people of his village. He sold everything else and gave the money to the poor. He made sure his sister was cared for by a group of good Christian women, and he started a new life of prayer.

[[scene:gospel]]

At first he lived near his village and learned from older holy men. From one he learned kindness, from another how to pray, from another how to be patient — like a bee collecting nectar from many flowers. He worked with his hands, shared what he earned, and prayed all day long.

### Light in the darkness

The devil hated that Anthony loved God so much. He tried to make Anthony give up by putting worries and bad thoughts in his mind. Anthony fought back by praying and fasting.

Later, Anthony went to live alone in an old empty building far from the village. At night the demons tried to scare him away. They made terrible noises and appeared like wild animals — lions, bears, wolves and snakes. But Anthony said, "If God has not given you power, you can't hurt me. My trust is in the Lord!"

[[scene:fortress]]

Suddenly a bright light shone down from above, and all the scary shapes disappeared. "Lord, where were You?" Anthony asked. A voice answered: "Anthony, I was here all along. I was watching you be brave. I will always help you."

### A city in the desert

Anthony moved even deeper into the desert, to an old abandoned fort, and lived there alone, praying, for twenty years! People heard about this holy man and came to see him. When at last he came out, they were amazed: he was healthy, calm and full of peace. He healed sick people, comforted sad people, and helped enemies make friends again.

Many young men wanted to live like Anthony, so they became monks and lived in the desert too. Soon the empty desert was filled with monks praying and working — it was like a city of God!

Anthony later moved to a mountain near the Red Sea, where there was a spring and some palm trees. He planted a garden. When wild animals came and trampled his vegetables, he kindly asked them to stop — and they listened!

### Dinner delivered by a raven

When Anthony was ninety years old, God told him that there was another hermit, even holier than he, living deep in the desert. Anthony walked a long way and found old Paul in a cave. They had never met, but they greeted each other by name, as if they were old friends.

[[scene:raven]]

As they talked about God, a raven flew down with a whole loaf of bread. "Look!" said Paul. "For sixty years this raven has brought me half a loaf every day. Today God sent a whole loaf, because you are my guest!"

### The Father of Monks

Anthony never went to school, but wise men came from far away to ask his advice. "My book," he told one of them, "is all of God's creation. It is always open in front of me."

Anthony lived to be 105 years old! People call him the "Father of Monks," because monasteries all over the world follow his example. St Anthony teaches us to listen to the Gospel as if Jesus is speaking to us, to be brave in the dark, and to trust God for everything. Holy Anthony, pray to God for us!
