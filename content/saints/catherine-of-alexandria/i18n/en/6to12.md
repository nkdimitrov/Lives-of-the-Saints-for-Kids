Long ago, in the great city of Alexandria in Egypt, lived a princess named Catherine. Alexandria had the biggest library in the world, and Catherine loved to read. She studied poetry, science, medicine and many languages, and she could out-argue the cleverest teachers in the city.

Catherine was also beautiful, and many princes wanted to marry her. But she said, "I will only marry someone who is as noble, rich, handsome and wise as I am." Nobody was good enough!

### The old man in the cave

Catherine's mother was a secret Christian. She took Catherine to visit a holy old hermit who lived in a cave outside the city. "I know someone far greater than you in every way," the hermit told her. "He is the King of all kings. His beauty is brighter than the sun, and His wisdom made the stars." He gave her an icon of the Virgin Mary holding the Child Jesus. "Pray to His Mother tonight," he said, "and ask to see her Son."

That night Catherine dreamed that she saw the Mother of God with little Jesus. But Jesus turned His face away. "I cannot look at her," He said, "because she does not know Me yet."

Catherine ran back to the hermit. He taught her all about Jesus — how He came to earth, died on the Cross and rose again — and then he baptized her.

[[scene:ring]]

The next night Catherine had another dream. This time little Jesus smiled at her, and He placed a ring on her finger. "Now you are Mine," He said. When Catherine woke up, the ring was really there! From then on, she gave her whole heart to Jesus.

### Fifty wise men

One day the Roman emperor came to Alexandria and ordered everyone to worship idols. Catherine marched right up to him and explained that idols made of stone and gold cannot be gods — only the Creator of everything is God!

The emperor couldn't answer her, so he called fifty of the wisest philosophers in his empire. "Prove this girl wrong," he ordered, "and I will make you rich."

[[scene:philosophers]]

The philosophers laughed at first. But Catherine prayed, and then she answered every one of their arguments. One by one they fell silent. At last their leader said, "She is right. Her God is the true God!" And all fifty became Christians. The emperor was so angry that he had them put to death, but they went to heaven as martyrs.

### The wheel breaks

The emperor put Catherine in prison and gave her no food. But a white dove came every day with food in its beak! The empress herself secretly visited Catherine in prison, and she believed in Jesus too, along with the captain of the guard and two hundred soldiers.

Then the emperor built a terrible machine — a big wheel with sharp spikes — to frighten and hurt Catherine. But as soon as she prayed, an angel came down and smashed the wheel to pieces!

[[scene:wheel]]

In the end, the emperor ordered Catherine to be put to death with the sword. She was not afraid — she was going to Jesus. The Church says that angels carried her body to the top of Mount Sinai, where Moses received the Ten Commandments. Today a famous monastery there carries her name.

St Catherine teaches us that the best use of a clever mind is to know and love God, and that we should speak the truth bravely. Holy Catherine, pray to God for us!
