Catherine was a princess. She was very clever and read lots of books.

[[scene:ring]]

A holy old man told her about Jesus. Catherine was baptized. In a dream, little Jesus gave her a ring. Now she belonged to Jesus forever!

[[scene:philosophers]]

A bad emperor brought fifty wise men to argue with Catherine. But Catherine told them all about God — and all fifty believed in Jesus!

[[scene:wheel]]

The emperor wanted to hurt Catherine with a big wheel. But God sent an angel — *crack!* — and the wheel broke into pieces.

Catherine loved Jesus more than anything. Holy Catherine, pray for us!
