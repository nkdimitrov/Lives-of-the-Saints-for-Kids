Bartholomew was a little boy who lived in a wooden village with big forests. He tried and tried, but he could not learn to read.

[[scene:oak]]

One day he met a holy old man under an oak tree. The old man prayed and gave him a piece of holy bread. Then — Bartholomew could read!

When he grew up, he became a monk named Sergius. He lived in the forest and built a little wooden church.

[[scene:bear]]

A big bear came out of the trees. Sergius was not afraid. He shared his bread with the bear, and the bear came back every day to visit!

[[scene:blessing]]

Many people came to learn from Sergius. Even the prince came to ask for his blessing. Sergius said, "Go, God will help you!"

Holy Sergius, pray for us!
