George was a brave soldier. He loved Jesus with all his heart.

[[scene:confession]]

The emperor said, "Do not love Jesus!" But George stood up tall and said, "I belong to Jesus!"

[[scene:angel]]

The emperor was angry and put George in prison. But George was not alone. God sent a bright angel to comfort him and make him strong.

[[scene:dragon]]

A story about St George tells of a scary dragon by a lake. George came on his white horse, prayed, and beat the dragon. The princess was safe, and everyone thanked God!

St George, brave soldier of Christ, pray for us!
