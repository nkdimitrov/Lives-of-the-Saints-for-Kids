George was born a long time ago in a land called Cappadocia. His family loved Jesus. When George was still a boy, his father died for his faith, and his mother took him to live in the Holy Land.

George grew up tall, strong and brave. He became a soldier in the Roman army, and he was so good at leading others that he became an officer, a commander of a thousand men. Even the emperor, Diocletian, liked him very much.

### The emperor's terrible law

But Diocletian did not love God. One day he called his advisers together and made a cruel law: every Christian must worship the idols or be killed.

George knew he could not hide his faith. He gave all his money to the poor, set his servants free, and walked straight into the emperor's palace.

[[scene:confession]]

In front of the emperor and all his nobles, George said in a loud voice: "Your idols are not gods. Jesus Christ is the true God. I am a Christian!"

The whole room went silent. The emperor tried to change George's mind. "I will make you rich and important," he promised. But George answered, "Nothing in the world is worth more than Jesus."

### Strong in prison

The angry emperor threw George into prison. His soldiers hurt George in many cruel ways, hoping he would give up. But George prayed and trusted God. One night, when he was badly hurt, a voice from heaven said, "Do not be afraid, George. I am with you!"

[[scene:angel]]

An angel came, full of light, and healed him. When people saw George walk out healthy and strong, many of them believed in Jesus — even some soldiers, and even the emperor's wife, Empress Alexandra!

God did many miracles through George. He prayed for a poor farmer whose ox had died, and the ox came back to life. A magician tried to poison him, but George was not harmed, and the magician became a Christian.

At last the emperor took George to the temple of the idols, hoping he would bow down. Instead George made the sign of the cross, and all the statues crashed to the ground! The emperor ordered George to be put to death. George was not afraid. He went to heaven as a martyr, a brave witness for Christ, on April 23.

### The dragon by the lake

Christians tell a famous story about a miracle of St George. Near a certain city lived a terrible dragon in a lake. The people were so afraid that they gave it their children, and one day it was the turn of the king's own daughter.

[[scene:dragon]]

As the princess waited by the lake, crying, a shining rider on a white horse appeared. It was St George! He made the sign of the cross, called on the name of Jesus, and struck the dragon with his spear. The princess tied her belt around its neck and led it into the city, tame as a puppy. "Believe in Jesus Christ, who sent me to save you," said George. And the king and all the people were baptized.

That is why icons show St George on a white horse defeating a dragon: it shows that with Christ, good always wins over evil.

St George teaches us to be brave, to tell the truth about our faith even when it is hard, and to help people who are in danger. Holy George, pray to God for us!
