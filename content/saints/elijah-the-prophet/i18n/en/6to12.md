Almost 3,000 years ago, long before Jesus was born, there lived a prophet named Elijah. A prophet is someone God chooses to speak His words to the people. Elijah wore a rough cloak made of sheepskin, lived simply in the wilderness, and loved God with a fiery love.

### The king who forgot God

At that time the people of Israel had a wicked king named Ahab and an even more wicked queen named Jezebel. They built temples to a false god called Baal and made the people worship him. They even hunted down God's prophets.

Elijah marched up to King Ahab and said: "As the Lord God lives, there will be no dew and no rain in this land until I say so!" And from that day, not a drop of rain fell for three and a half years. The fields dried up, and there was a great famine.

### Fed by ravens

God told Elijah to hide by a little stream called Cherith. "Drink from the stream," God said, "and I will send ravens to feed you." Every morning and every evening, black ravens flew down carrying bread and meat for Elijah!

[[scene:ravens]]

When the stream dried up, God sent Elijah to a poor widow. She had only a tiny bit of flour and oil left — just enough for one last meal for herself and her son. "Don't be afraid," Elijah told her. "Make me a little bread first, and God will make sure your flour and oil never run out." She trusted him — and for the rest of the famine her jar of flour and her jug of oil were never empty! Later, when her son got sick and died, Elijah prayed, and God brought the boy back to life.

### Fire from heaven

After three years, Elijah called all the people, and the 450 prophets of Baal, to Mount Carmel. "How long will you go back and forth between God and Baal?" he asked. "Let's have a test. The prophets of Baal will build an altar and pray to Baal. I will build an altar and pray to the Lord. The God who answers with fire — He is the true God!"

The prophets of Baal shouted and danced around their altar all day long: "Baal, answer us!" Nothing happened. "Shout louder!" Elijah teased. "Maybe Baal is asleep!" Still nothing.

Then Elijah built an altar of twelve stones. He even had the people pour twelve big jars of water all over it, until it was soaked. Then he prayed: "Lord, show everyone today that You are God!"

[[scene:carmel]]

**WHOOSH!** Fire fell from heaven and burned up everything — the wood, the stones, even the water! The people fell on their faces and shouted, "The Lord is God! The Lord is God!" Then Elijah climbed to the top of the mountain and prayed for rain. A tiny cloud, as small as a hand, rose from the sea — and soon there was a huge rainstorm.

### God in a gentle whisper

Queen Jezebel was furious and wanted to kill Elijah. He ran far into the desert and felt very sad and tired. But an angel brought him bread and water and said, "Get up and eat." Elijah walked for forty days to God's mountain.

There a mighty wind blew — but God was not in the wind. Then an earthquake shook the ground — but God was not in the earthquake. Then came a fire — but God was not in the fire. And then came a soft, gentle whisper of a breeze. That was where Elijah met God! God comforted him and gave him a helper: a young farmer named Elisha.

### The chariot of fire

When the time came for Elijah to go to heaven, he and Elisha walked to the river Jordan. Elijah struck the water with his cloak, and the river parted so they could cross on dry ground!

"What can I do for you before I go?" Elijah asked. "Please give me a double share of your spirit," said Elisha.

[[scene:chariot]]

Suddenly a chariot of fire, pulled by horses of fire, swept between them, and Elijah was carried up into heaven in a whirlwind! Elisha caught Elijah's cloak as it fell, and he became a great prophet too.

Elijah never died — God took him straight to heaven. Much later he appeared with Moses next to Jesus when Jesus shone with light on Mount Tabor. Elijah teaches us to love God with all our heart and to listen for Him in quiet prayer. Holy Prophet Elijah, pray to God for us!
