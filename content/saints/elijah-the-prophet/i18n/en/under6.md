Elijah was a prophet. That means he spoke God's words to the people.

A bad king made everyone pray to a fake god. So Elijah said, "No rain will fall!" And no rain fell for a long time.

[[scene:ravens]]

Elijah hid by a little stream. Every morning and every evening, black birds called ravens brought him bread to eat. God took care of him!

[[scene:carmel]]

On a big mountain, Elijah prayed, "Lord, show everyone that You are the true God!" *Whoosh!* Fire came down from heaven. All the people shouted, "The Lord is God!" And then the rain came.

[[scene:chariot]]

At the end, God sent a chariot of fire with fiery horses. It carried Elijah up, up, up to heaven!

Holy Prophet Elijah, pray for us!
