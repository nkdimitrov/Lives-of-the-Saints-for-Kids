Conon was a little boy who lived in a village high up in the mountains.

[[scene:archangel]]

One day a shining angel came to him. It was the Archangel Michael! The angel taught Conon about Jesus and baptized him. "I will always help you," said the angel.

When Conon grew up, he married a girl named Anna. On their wedding night he put a candle under a pot, and the room went dark.

[[scene:candle]]

"Which is better, light or darkness?" he asked. "Light!" said Anna. Conon lifted the pot. "Jesus is the Light," he said. Anna loved Jesus too, and soon both their families did.

Conon prayed for sick people, and God made them well. Bad spirits ran away from him.

A bad governor hurt Conon because he loved Jesus. But all the people of his town came running to save him!

[[scene:rescue]]

Conon thanked God, and later he went to be with Jesus in peace.

Holy Conon, pray for us!
