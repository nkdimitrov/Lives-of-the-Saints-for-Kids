Basil loved to learn. He and his best friend Gregory went to school together in a faraway city called Athens.

[[scene:athens]]

They only walked on two roads: the road to church and the road to school!

[[scene:famine]]

When Basil grew up, he became a bishop. One year there was no food, and people were hungry. Basil cooked big pots of soup and gave it to everyone with his own hands.

[[scene:modestus]]

A grumpy ruler shouted at Basil, "I will take away everything you have!" Basil smiled and said, "I only have God — and nobody can take Him away!"

On New Year's Day we remember St Basil. Holy Basil, pray for us!
