Constantine was an emperor — a very important king. His mother's name was Helen.

[[scene:vision]]

One day Constantine looked up and saw a shining Cross in the sky! He heard the words: "With this sign, you will win!" Constantine became a friend of Jesus. He made a law: "Christians can pray to God and nobody can hurt them!"

[[scene:cross]]

His mother Helen went on a long trip to Jerusalem. She looked and looked for the Cross of Jesus — and she found it! When a sick lady touched the Cross, she got well.

[[scene:city]]

Constantine built a beautiful new city by the sea, full of churches. He called it Constantinople.

Holy Constantine and Helen, pray for us!
