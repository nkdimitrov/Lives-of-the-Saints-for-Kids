About 1,700 years ago, the Roman Empire was the biggest empire in the world. For almost three hundred years its emperors had hurt and killed Christians. But that was about to change — because of a boy named Constantine and his mother, Helen.

Constantine's father was a Roman general who was fair and kind, and who would not hurt Christians. His mother, Helen, came from a simple family. When Constantine grew up, he saw how bravely the Christians suffered. After his father died, the soldiers made Constantine emperor.

### A Cross in the sky

But the empire had other rulers too, and they fought each other. A cruel tyrant named Maxentius ruled in Rome, and he had a much bigger army than Constantine. Constantine was worried. He prayed to the one true God to help him.

One day at noon, as his army marched, Constantine looked up — and saw a huge, shining Cross of light in the sky! Around it were the words: **"By this sign, conquer!"** His whole army saw it too.

[[scene:vision]]

That night Jesus appeared to Constantine in a dream and told him to put the sign of the Cross on his army's banners. Constantine had a golden banner made with the Cross and the first letters of Christ's name, and he painted crosses on his soldiers' shields. In the battle at the Milvian Bridge, outside Rome, Constantine won a great victory.

### Christians are free!

In the year 313 Constantine made a new law: Christians were free to worship God! The persecutions stopped. The prisons opened, and the Christians who had been hiding came out to pray in the open. Constantine gave back the churches that had been taken, built new ones, helped the poor and made Sunday a day of rest.

Constantine also called all the bishops of the world to a great meeting in the city of Nicaea, so they could clearly say what Christians believe. St Nicholas was there! The bishops wrote the Creed that we still say in church.

### Helen finds the Cross

Constantine's mother, Helen, became a Christian too, and she loved Jesus very much. Even though she was almost eighty years old, she made a long journey to Jerusalem to find the Cross of Jesus.

That wasn't easy! The Romans had covered the place where Jesus was crucified with dirt and built a temple to an idol on top. Helen had the temple taken down and the ground dug up. Deep underground, the workers found the tomb of Jesus — and three wooden crosses!

[[scene:cross]]

But which one was Jesus' Cross? The bishop of Jerusalem, Macarius, prayed. Then they touched each cross to a very sick woman. Nothing happened with the first or the second. But when the third Cross touched her, she was healed and stood up! Everyone knew: this was the Cross of Christ!

So many people wanted to see it that the bishop lifted the Cross up high for everyone, and the crowd sang, "Lord, have mercy!" We remember this every year on September 14, the Elevation of the Cross. People say that sweet-smelling basil grew where the Cross was found, which is why we use basil in church on that day.

Helen built beautiful churches in Jerusalem and Bethlehem. She gave money to the poor and even served food to people with her own hands, like a humble servant.

### A new city

Constantine built a magnificent new capital city by the sea, full of churches and palaces. He gave it to the protection of the Mother of God and named it Constantinople — "the City of Constantine." Today it is called Istanbul.

[[scene:city]]

At the end of his life, Constantine was baptized. After that, he wouldn't wear his purple royal robes anymore — only white clothes, to show how happy he was to belong to Christ.

Sts Constantine and Helen are called "Equal-to-the-Apostles," because they helped the Gospel spread all over the world. In icons they stand together holding the Cross. Holy Constantine and Helen, pray to God for us!
