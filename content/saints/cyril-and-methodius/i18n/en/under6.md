Cyril and Methodius were brothers. They loved God, and they loved books!

Far away lived people called Slavs. They wanted to learn about Jesus, but they had no letters to write their language.

[[scene:alphabet]]

Cyril prayed to God for a long time. Then he made brand new letters — a whole alphabet — so the Slavs could read and write!

[[scene:moravia]]

The brothers went to the Slavs and read the Gospel to them in their own language. Everyone was so happy! "Now we understand!"

[[scene:rome]]

The Pope in Rome blessed their books, too.

Every time we read and write, we can thank God for Sts Cyril and Methodius. Holy Cyril and Methodius, pray for us!
