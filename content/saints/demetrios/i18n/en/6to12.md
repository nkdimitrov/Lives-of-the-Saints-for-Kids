Long ago, in Thessaloniki, a great city beside the sea, there lived a Roman governor and his wife. They were Christians, but they had to keep it secret because the emperor hated Christians. Inside their house they had a hidden room — a tiny church with icons and glowing lamps — where they prayed every day.

They prayed for a child, and God gave them a son. They named him Demetrios. When he was small, his father would take his hand, lead him into the secret chapel and tell him the stories of the saints in the icons.

### A brave young governor

When Demetrios grew up, his father died, and the emperor Maximian made Demetrios the new governor of Thessaloniki. "Your job," the emperor told him, "is to find the Christians and punish them!"

But Demetrios did something amazing. Instead of hunting Christians, he stood up in the city and taught everyone about Jesus! Many people listened and believed.

[[scene:teaching]]

When the emperor heard this, he was furious. Demetrios knew trouble was coming. He gave everything he owned to the poor with the help of his faithful servant, Lupus. When the emperor arrived, Demetrios said bravely, "Yes, I am a Christian," and he was thrown into a dark prison.

In the prison a scorpion crawled toward him. Demetrios made the sign of the cross, and the scorpion died. Then an angel appeared, shining with light, and said, "Peace to you! Be brave and strong."

### Nestor and the giant

The emperor loved to watch fights in the stadium. His champion was a giant named Lyaeus, huge and mean. He would throw people off a high platform onto sharp spears. Many Christians had died this way.

A young man named Nestor, who had learned about Jesus from Demetrios, couldn't stand it anymore. He went to the prison and said, "Demetrios, please pray for me. I am going to fight Lyaeus!"

Demetrios made the sign of the cross over him. "Go," he said. "With God's help you will win."

[[scene:nestor]]

The whole stadium laughed when they saw the slim young man facing the giant. But Nestor shouted, **"God of Demetrios, help me!"** — and he threw the mighty Lyaeus right off the platform! It was like David and Goliath. The crowd gasped. The emperor was so embarrassed and angry that he had Nestor put to death. When he learned that Demetrios had blessed Nestor, he sent soldiers to kill Demetrios in the prison too. Both of them went to heaven as martyrs.

### The protector of his city

Lupus saved Demetrios's robe and ring, and God healed sick people through them. Later a beautiful church was built over Demetrios's tomb, and a sweet-smelling oil called myrrh began to flow from his relics. That is why he is called the "Myrrh-streamer."

[[scene:protector]]

The people of Thessaloniki say that many times, when enemies attacked their city, they saw St Demetrios on the walls or riding a horse in front of the gates, and the enemies ran away.

St Demetrios teaches us to be brave about our faith wherever we are, and Nestor teaches us that even a small person can do great things with God's help. Holy Demetrios, pray to God for us!
