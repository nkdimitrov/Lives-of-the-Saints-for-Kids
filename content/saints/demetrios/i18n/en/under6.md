Demetrios lived in a big city by the sea called Thessaloniki. He loved Jesus very much.

[[scene:teaching]]

Demetrios was in charge of the city. He told everyone, "Jesus loves you!" Many people learned to love God.

[[scene:nestor]]

A huge, mean giant named Lyaeus was hurting Christians. A boy named Nestor said, "Bless me, Demetrios!" Then Nestor prayed, "God of Demetrios, help me!" — and little Nestor beat the giant!

[[scene:protector]]

Now Demetrios is with God in heaven. He still prays for his city and helps people who call on him.

Holy Demetrios, pray for us!
