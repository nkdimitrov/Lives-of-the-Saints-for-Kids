Long ago, in the mountains of Asia Minor, there was a city called Antioch of Pisidia. (It is a different Antioch from the famous one in Syria!) There lived a pagan priest who served the idols in the temple. He had a baby daughter named Marina.

Sadly, Marina's mother died when she was very small. Her father did not know how to care for a baby, so he sent her to a nurse who lived in a village in the hills. The nurse was a secret Christian. She loved Marina like her own child, and as Marina grew up, she told her all about Jesus: how He made the world, how He loved us so much that He died on the Cross for us, and how He rose again.

She also told her stories about the martyrs — brave Christians who would not stop loving Jesus even when wicked rulers tried to make them. "I want to be brave like them," Marina said.

When her father found out that his daughter had become a Christian, he was furious, and he would not have anything more to do with her. But Marina stayed with her nurse, and she was happy.

### The shepherd girl

Marina helped her nurse by looking after the sheep. Every day she led them out to the green hillsides, where they nibbled the grass among the flowers and the olive trees. While the sheep grazed, Marina prayed and sang psalms to God.

[[scene:sheep]]

One day, when Marina was fifteen, the governor of the province, a proud man named Olybrius, was riding along the road to Antioch. He saw Marina with her sheep and thought she was the loveliest girl he had ever seen. "Bring her to me," he told his soldiers. "If she is free-born, I will marry her!"

When Marina was brought before him, he asked who she was. "My name is Marina," she said, "and I am a Christian."

### Before the governor

The next day the governor sat on his judgment seat in the city. "Forget this Jesus," he said. "Bow down to our gods, and I will make you my wife. You will live in a palace and wear jewels and silk!"

"I already have a Bridegroom," answered Marina. "He is Jesus Christ, and I will never leave Him. Your idols are made of stone and wood. They cannot see or hear or help anyone."

Olybrius was very angry. He had Marina beaten, but she did not give in. Then he threw her into prison.

### The dragon in the dark

That night, in the dark cell, Marina was praying when something terrible happened. The devil came to frighten her — in the shape of a huge, dark dragon with fiery eyes! It hissed and opened its jaws as if to swallow her.

Marina was afraid for a moment. But she remembered that Jesus is stronger than any evil. She made the sign of the Cross, and at once the dragon vanished like smoke.

Then the devil came again in another shape. This time Marina caught hold of him and threw him to the ground. That is why, in Greek icons, St Marina is shown with a hammer in her hand, standing over the defeated demon!

[[scene:dragon]]

Suddenly the whole prison was filled with light. Marina saw a great shining Cross, and on top of it sat a white dove. "Rejoice, Marina," said a voice, "for you have won the victory!" And all the wounds from her beating were healed.

### The dove with the crown

In the morning, the soldiers brought Marina back to the governor. Everyone gasped. She wasn't hurt at all — her face was shining with joy!

Olybrius tried new tortures, but nothing could shake Marina's faith. At last he ordered his men to tie her up and throw her into a huge tub full of water.

Marina prayed, "Lord, let this water be my holy baptism!" At that moment the earth shook, and a dove came down from heaven carrying a golden crown. The ropes fell from Marina's hands, and she stood up in the water, safe and full of light.

[[scene:dove]]

The people of Antioch who saw this cried out, "The God of Marina is the true God!" Thousands of them came to believe in Christ that day. The governor was so angry that he had them put to death, but they all went to heaven as martyrs.

### Going home to Jesus

In the end, Olybrius ordered that Marina should be put to death with the sword. Before she died, Marina prayed for everyone who would ever remember her and ask for her help. Then she bowed her head, and went joyfully to Jesus, whom she loved more than anything in the world. She was only fifteen years old.

Today St Marina is loved all over the Orthodox world, especially in Greece, where many churches are named after her. In the West she is called St Margaret. People ask for her prayers when they are frightened or in trouble, because she teaches us that the devil runs away from the Cross of Christ.

Holy Marina, brave shepherd girl, pray to God for us!
