Marina lived long ago with a kind nurse in a little house in the hills. Her nurse taught her all about Jesus, and Marina loved Him with all her heart.

[[scene:sheep]]

Marina looked after the sheep. One day a proud governor rode by on his horse. "Marry me," he said, "and bow down to my idols!" "No," said Marina. "I belong to Jesus."

The governor was angry, and he put Marina in a dark prison.

[[scene:dragon]]

In the night the devil came, looking like a scary dragon. But Marina was not afraid. She made the sign of the Cross — and the dragon ran away! Then a shining Cross appeared, with a white dove on top.

[[scene:dove]]

The governor threw Marina into a big tub of water. But a dove came down from heaven with a golden crown. Marina was safe, and many, many people believed in Jesus!

In icons, St Marina holds a hammer, because she chased the devil away. Holy Marina, pray for us!
