Pantoleon was a young doctor. He knew lots of medicines.

[[scene:hermolaus]]

A kind old priest told him, "Jesus is the best Doctor of all! He can heal anyone."

[[scene:child]]

One day Pantoleon saw a little boy who had been bitten by a snake. He prayed, "Jesus, please help him!" And the boy got up, alive and well! Pantoleon was baptized and loved Jesus.

He helped sick people and never asked for money. He healed a blind man too!

[[scene:beasts]]

A bad emperor sent lions and bears to hurt him. But the wild animals lay down gently at his feet. God gave him a new name: Panteleimon, which means "very kind to everyone."

Holy Panteleimon, pray for us!
