About 1,700 years ago, in a big city called Nicomedia, lived a boy named Pantoleon. His father was a rich, important man who worshipped the Roman idols. His mother was a Christian who taught him to pray, but sadly she died when he was young.

Pantoleon was very smart. He decided to become a doctor and studied with the emperor's own physician. Soon he was the best student of all, and even the emperor wanted him to be his doctor.

### The true Doctor

Every day, on his way to class, Pantoleon walked past a little house where an old priest named Hermolaus was hiding from the Christian-hating emperor. One day Hermolaus invited him in.

"You are learning to heal the body," the old priest said kindly. "But there is a Doctor greater than all the doctors in the world: Jesus Christ. He made the blind see and raised the dead. If you believe in Him, you can heal people in His name."

[[scene:hermolaus]]

Pantoleon remembered his mother's prayers, and his heart was warmed. He began to visit Hermolaus often.

### The boy and the snake

One day, walking along a road, Pantoleon found a little boy lying still on the ground. A poisonous snake had bitten him, and the snake was still there. Pantoleon thought, "Now I will see if what Hermolaus said is true." He looked up to heaven and prayed: "Lord Jesus Christ, let this child live!"

[[scene:child]]

The boy sat up, alive and well — and the snake burst apart! Pantoleon was so happy that he ran to Hermolaus and asked to be baptized.

### Free medicine for everyone

Soon afterwards a blind man came to Pantoleon's house. He had spent all his money on doctors, but none could help him. Pantoleon touched his eyes and said, "In the name of Jesus Christ, see!" — and the man could see! When Pantoleon's father saw this miracle, he believed in Jesus too.

After his father died, Pantoleon set his servants free and gave his money to the poor. He visited sick people everywhere — in rich houses, poor houses, even prisons — and healed them all in Jesus' name. And he never charged anyone a single coin!

### The jealous doctors

The other doctors in the city were jealous because everyone went to Pantoleon. They told the emperor that he was a Christian. The emperor ordered him to worship the idols. Instead, Pantoleon said, "Let's have a test. Bring a sick man. Let your priests pray to your gods, and I will pray to mine."

A paralyzed man was carried in. The pagan priests prayed all day, but nothing happened. Then Pantoleon prayed, took the man's hand and said, "In the name of Jesus Christ, get up!" — and the man stood up and walked! Many people believed in Jesus. But the emperor was furious.

The emperor tried to hurt Pantoleon in many ways, but each time God protected him. At last he threw him to the wild animals in the arena.

[[scene:beasts]]

But the lions, leopards and bears did not attack. They lay down at his feet like friendly puppies and licked his hands! The crowd shouted, "Great is the God of the Christians!"

### "All-merciful"

In the end the emperor ordered Pantoleon to be put to death by the sword, tied to an olive tree. At first the sword bent like soft wax! Then a voice came from heaven and gave him a new name: **Panteleimon**, which means "all-merciful," because he showed God's mercy to everyone. Panteleimon was ready to go to Jesus, and he forgave the soldiers. When he died, the old olive tree suddenly blossomed and was covered with olives.

In icons, St Panteleimon holds a little medicine box and spoon. Christians pray to him when someone is sick. He teaches us to use our talents to help others, and to do it with kindness. Holy Panteleimon, pray to God for us!
