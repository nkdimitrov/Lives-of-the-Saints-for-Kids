Mary lived in a big city. She did many wrong things and did not think about God at all.

[[scene:icon]]

One day Mary went to a church in Jerusalem, but she could not go in! She cried and prayed to the Mother of God: "Please help me. I want to be good." Then she could go in, and she knew God loved her.

Mary went to live in the quiet desert. She prayed and said sorry to God for many, many years. God made her heart clean and full of light.

[[scene:jordan]]

When Mary was very old, a monk named Zosimas brought her Holy Communion. Mary walked across the river on the water to meet him!

[[scene:lion]]

When Mary went to heaven, a kind lion helped Zosimas make her grave.

St Mary shows us that God always forgives us when we say sorry. Holy Mary, pray for us!
