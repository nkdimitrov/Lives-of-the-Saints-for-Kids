This is the story of a woman who was very far from God, and how God brought her back home.

### A monk meets a stranger in the desert

Near the river Jordan there was a monastery with a special custom. During Lent the monks would cross the river and each go alone into the desert to pray for forty days. One of them was an old priest-monk named Zosimas.

On his twentieth day in the desert, Zosimas saw a person in the distance — very thin, burnt dark by the sun, with short white hair. The person ran away, but Zosimas followed, calling, "Servant of God, wait!"

"Abba Zosimas," the stranger called back, "I am a woman, and my clothes have worn away. Please throw me your cloak." Zosimas was amazed: she knew his name, though they had never met! He threw her his cloak, and she came and spoke with him. When she prayed, Zosimas saw her lifted up a little above the ground! He begged her to tell him who she was.

### Mary's story

"My name is Mary," she said. "I was born in Egypt. When I was twelve, I ran away from home to the big city of Alexandria. For seventeen years I lived a very selfish and sinful life. I didn't care about God or about anyone else — only about having fun for myself.

"One day I saw crowds of pilgrims sailing to Jerusalem for the Feast of the Holy Cross. I went along, but not to pray — just for adventure.

[[scene:icon]]

"On the feast day, everyone went into the church to see the Cross. But when I tried to go in, an invisible power stopped me at the door! I tried again and again, but I couldn't get in. Then I understood: my sins were keeping me out.

"I began to cry. Above me I saw an icon of the Virgin Mary. I prayed to her: 'Mother of God, I am not worthy even to look at you. But please help me! Let me go in and see the Cross of your Son, and I promise I will change my life.'

"Then I tried the door again — and I walked right in! I kissed the holy Cross, and my heart was filled with joy. I heard a voice say, *'Cross the Jordan, and you will find rest.'* So I went to the river, received Holy Communion, crossed over, and I have lived in this desert ever since — for forty-seven years."

Mary told Zosimas that for the first seventeen years it was very hard. She missed her old life and was tempted to go back. But every time, she remembered her promise and prayed to the Mother of God, who always helped her. At last her heart became peaceful and clean.

### Walking on the water

Mary asked Zosimas to come back the next year on Holy Thursday and bring her Holy Communion. A year later he waited on the riverbank with the chalice. When Mary appeared on the other side, there was no boat. But Mary made the sign of the cross over the river — and walked across on top of the water!

[[scene:jordan]]

She received Holy Communion with great joy and said, "Lord, now I can go in peace." Then she walked back across the river.

### The helpful lion

The next year Zosimas went to find Mary again. He found that she had died peacefully, and that she had written a message in the sand asking him to bury her. But the ground was hard, and Zosimas was old and had no shovel. Suddenly a big lion appeared! Zosimas was frightened, but the lion was gentle. "Please help me dig," said Zosimas, and the lion dug a grave with its paws. Then it went quietly back into the desert.

[[scene:lion]]

Zosimas went home and told everyone about St Mary. We remember her on April 1, and also on the fifth Sunday of Lent. Her life teaches us that **no one is too far away for God's love.** If we are sorry and turn back to Him, He always welcomes us home. Holy Mary, pray to God for us!
